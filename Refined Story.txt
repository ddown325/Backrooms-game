It was 2026. Rain slammed against the rocky pavement, reflecting the blinding glare of passing cars that made my head throb. The air felt thick and musty, an eerie smell of wet earth and grass rising up in the middle of such a crowded place. 

Then, a sudden wave of nausea hit me. A foul stench of wet fur and rotting breath filled the air. Heavy non human footsteps started echoing right behind me. My heart sank into my chest, and my legs turned to jelly. I’d better hurry, I thought, quickening my pace. Maybe I was losing my mind, but I was convinced a creature was stalking me. 

I picked up the pace, my vision blurring into smudges of bright light as panic set in. A deep, low growl vibrated through the air, forcing me to glance back. Cold, yellow eyes locked onto mine. Its teeth were yellowed and rotten, stained with fresh red. An acrid stench of decay hit me like a wall. The creature was too massive to see all at once, yet it was terrifyingly close. Was it human? No, that couldn’t be right. It was a fleshy mass of unnatural clicks, pouring pure dread into my soul.

I ducked into a pitch black alleyway. The walls were slick with wet slime and moss, and the uneven, broken floor tiles made me stumble. My breath came in short, ragged gasps. This is it, I thought. I could still smell the monster's foul decay, it was right on top of me. 

Just my luck: a dead end. 

A large, dark green dumpster blocked the path. My chest tightened as my heart hammered against my ribs. Desperate to hide, I dove inside, hoping for safety, but I slammed hard into the trash. The reek of dirt and detergent filled my nose as sharp glass sliced open my palm, leaving a searing, red hot pain and a hand covered in blood.

A ferocious growl shattered the quiet as the monster slammed against the alley walls, shaking everything around me. I winced as the glass dug deeper into my hand. I knew this was the end. In pure panic, I scrambled back out of the dumpster. 

I froze. Standing before me was a nightmare made of drought and hell, its limbs bent unnaturally, bones morphed all wrong. Before I could even run, the creature threw me against the metal dumpster wall. The impact shattered my ribs, sending blood spraying from my mouth. Driven by pure, unadulterated terror and unable to face what it would do to me, I grabbed the jagged glass in my hand and slashed it across my own throat, choking as blood filled my lungs...
