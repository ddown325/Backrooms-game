HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE
Recover the three tape reels of the evacuation broadcast, then reach the service door at the far end of the floor and open it. The reels glow faintly gold and hum with static.

THE SIGNAL METER
The meter (top left, under your tasks) tracks the nearest reel like a Geiger counter: the bar fills and the pings quicken as you get closer, and the arrow shows which way to turn. Once all three reels are in, the meter switches to the service door.

COHERENCE
Your sanity. It drains in dim or dark areas and recovers under working lights. Almond water restores a large amount. At zero you become part of the hum.

THE LISTENER
A tall pale figure that walks toward you whenever you cannot see it — or whenever it stands in the dark. Looking at it, while it is lit, freezes it, but staring costs coherence. The torch makes it flinch and retreat. If it reaches you, you lose coherence and it vanishes for a while.

THE CRAWLER
A low, many-legged shape that sprints across the corridors ahead of you. It does not chase — it is just passing through. Step back from its path; brushing it costs coherence. The torch makes it hurry.

THE WARDEN
On long shifts a heavy silhouette patrols a fixed loop, dragging something behind it. It cannot be frozen by looking — only avoided. Stay out of its gaze, break line of sight, and let it pass. It appears as a red mark on the floor plan when it is near.

THE MIMICS
Some mannequins are staffed by something else. They stand a little too still, and if you watch one long enough it will notice. Do not stare. Do not stand close.

LIGHTS & BREAKERS
Some tubes are dead and some flicker. Grey breaker boxes on the walls (SPACE) restore every dead tube nearby. Power cuts black everything out for a few seconds.

THE TORCH
F toggles the torch. It drains a battery; pick up cells to recharge. It lets you see, but only the building’s own lights heal you.

SHIFTING WALLS
When you are not looking, corridors rearrange. You will hear a low tone first. The reels and the exit always remain reachable.

NOTES
Read notes and signs with SPACE or click. Everything you find is saved in the Archive.

CONTROLS
WASD move · mouse look · SHIFT creep · Q/E lean · F torch · SPACE or click use · M floor plan · ESC pause. Gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, A use, X torch, Y map, bumpers lean, B creep, Start pause.
